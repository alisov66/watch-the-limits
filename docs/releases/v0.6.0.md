The reviewed Figma panel now includes available Codex resets, inline confirmation, and recovery when a reset's outcome is uncertain.

## What's new

- **Available resets:** review your available reset count, scope, and expiration. Loading, no available resets, and stale information have distinct states.
- **Inline reset confirmation:** choose **Use reset**, then **Confirm reset**. Only one confirmation is active, with no automatic confirmation timeout. Escape closes the panel and cancels an unconfirmed selection.
- **Background completion:** a submitted reset continues if the panel closes or its section collapses. Other reset actions are disabled while processing.
- **Immediate reconciliation:** usage and reset availability refresh after the attempt. A timeout is treated as an unknown outcome; retrying the selected reset reuses its saved retry key to avoid another logical redemption.
- **Clear recovery messages:** row-level reset errors remain visible during confirmation and clear when submitting another attempt, closing the panel, or collapsing Available resets.
- **Remembered sections:** Settings and Available resets start collapsed and independently remember expansion across panel openings and app restarts.
- **Updated panel components:** typography, spacing, counters, and control states follow the reviewed design. Native macOS glass and switch appearance are preserved. Stale or unavailable menu-bar values remain **—**.

Reset availability depends on the information returned by your Codex version and account. When reset information is unavailable, the section shows **No data**; this is different from having no available resets.

## Install or update

1. Quit any running copy of **Watch the Limits**.
2. Download **Watch-the-Limits-macOS.zip** from Assets below and unzip it.
3. Move **Watch the Limits.app** to Applications, replacing the previous copy, then open it.

Requires **macOS 13 or newer**. The download supports **Apple Silicon and Intel**, with native Liquid Glass on macOS 26 and later. Codex must be installed locally and signed in with your ChatGPT account.

This release is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks it, review the release and use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**, when available.
